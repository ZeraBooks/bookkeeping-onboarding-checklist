# What the first month looks like

A remote bookkeeping engagement usually runs like this for the first month.
Later months are shorter, because the questions from month one have answers.

## Week 1: documents in

- The owner sends the statements and documents on `document-request.csv`.
- The bookkeeper checks every account has every month and asks for the gaps.
- Opening balances come from the last trusted trial balance, or from the
  statements if there is none.

## Week 2: categorize and ask

- Every transaction is categorized against the chart of accounts.
- Anything the bookkeeper can't tell from the statement goes to the owner as a
  plain question: personal or business, what a transfer was for, which invoice
  a deposit paid.
- Expect more questions this month than any month after.

## Week 3: reconcile

- Each bank and card account is reconciled to its printed closing balance.
- Transfers between accounts are paired, so they aren't counted as income or
  expense.
- Loan payments are split into interest and principal from the loan
  statement.

## Week 4: close and report

- Month-end entries are posted: depreciation, prepaid costs and anything paid
  for several months at once.
- The month is closed and the owner gets a profit and loss, balance sheet and
  cash flow.
- The owner reads the reports and raises anything that looks wrong.

## If the books were behind

Catch-up months are done oldest first, each tied to its statement before the
next one starts. Ongoing monthly work begins once the catch-up reaches the
current month.
